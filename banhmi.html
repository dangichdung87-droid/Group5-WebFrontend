Banh Mi

A Vietnamese Street Food Icon

Crispy Savory Fresh

About Banh Mi

Banh Mi is one of Vietnam's most famous street foods.

The combination of different textures and flavors makes Banh Mi simple but unique.

Today, Banh Mi can be found throughout Vietnam and has also become popular in many countries around the world.

Origin: Vietnam
Type: Street Food

Main Ingredients

  • Vietnamese baguette
  • Grilled pork or Vietnamese sausage
  • Pate
  • Cucumber
  • Pickled carrot and daikon
  • Coriander
  • Chili and sauce

Special Flavor

Banh Mi has a crispy crust and a soft inside. The combination of savory meat, rich pate, fresh vegetables and herbs creates a balanced flavor.

Some versions are spicy, while others are sweet and savory. Each region of Vietnam can have its own style of Banh Mi.

A Short History

The baguette was introduced to Vietnam during the French colonial period. Vietnamese people later adapted it by adding local ingredients such as pate, pork, pickled vegetables and fresh herbs. Over time, Banh Mi became an important part of Vietnamese street food culture.
